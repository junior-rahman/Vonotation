Voitation Android 0.1.20-preview (92)

- Polished Sand & Cocoa Settings, saved-text search, Recently deleted and recovery cards, with clearer spacing and large-text layouts.
- The approved V-wave and selected Thought Loop now appear throughout dictation, Quick Thoughts and saved-content controls. Native vectors follow the recording and processing states.
- When a dictation loses its input and successfully copies its transcript, Clipboard pops out of the floating fan. Tap its preview to open the saved text. Ordinary copies, inserted text, private dictation and failed clipboard writes do not trigger it. No text is automatically pasted.
- The copied-text preview dismisses after 12 seconds or when dismissed/locked; sensitive fields suppress it.

Install this APK over the existing Voitation app. The package and original signing certificate are retained; do not uninstall to update.

Verification: both Android products build and lint; 43 Java suites; native recovery/storage checks; light/dark production screens, 160% text and unfolded layouts; native artwork rendering and real Android clipboard fallback/privacy checks and isolated fan-trigger policy checks. Physical-device microphone, provider and cross-app input-loss flows remain untested. Artwork-state sheets are native view fixtures, not complete app screens. iOS testing and backup/OAuth remain paused.
