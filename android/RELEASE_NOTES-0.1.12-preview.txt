VonoTation 0.1.12-preview
Android package: app.voiceflow
Version code: 84

Quick thoughts floating control
- With no input active, tap the idle dot or microphone to record in place.
- Hold it to open the saved Quick thoughts card inside the app.
- Drag it freely within the usable screen. It remembers the microphone's center and shares that position with the active-input controls, without snapping to an edge.
- Tap, hold and drag are separate gestures. Releasing a hold or drag does not start recording. Cancelled or multi-finger gestures do not activate the button.
- Idle collapse and repositioning pause while touching the control. Focus changes, hiding the control and teardown cancel delayed holds.
- Added the accessible Open Quick thoughts long-click action.
- Opening Thoughts works for both a new app launch and an existing app window. Selected cards survive activity recreation, including folding or rotation.
- Existing design, transparency, offline source-audio capture, summaries and hidden playback are retained. A recording still continues across outside taps until Finish or Cancel; system interruptions may finalize it.
