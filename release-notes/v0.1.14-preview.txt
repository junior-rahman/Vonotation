VonoTation 0.1.14-preview
Android package: app.voiceflow
Version code: 86

Short Quick thought captures
- New Quick thought captures are skipped only after a fully successful, complete transcription measures fewer than two normal phone-width lines. Active-input dictation is not filtered, and no existing saved thoughts are removed.
- Measurement uses a fixed 320-pixel width and 17-pixel text with whitespace collapsed, independent of screen folding, density and font scale. Original text in retained thoughts is unchanged.
- Offline captures remain pending until a transcript is available. Older pending recordings and explicit manual Retry/Save actions keep their previous behavior. Interrupted, recovered, uncertain or incomplete captures, including missing segments, are preserved.
- A skipped new capture's metadata and skip tombstone must commit before its source audio is deleted. Failed persistence preserves the audio. Retained thoughts keep their source recording and existing hidden Source audio playback.

The existing Daybook daily timeline, optional Notes-model daily recaps, detailed thought summaries, full transcripts and source playback remain unchanged. Tap-to-record, hold-to-open saved thoughts, free dragging, offline recording, the compact clipboard, cursor-aware deletion, quiet Call Notes recovery, backup controls and in-app updates remain included. There is no design change.
